## 1. Conventions (recommended)

### 1.1 Hungarian personal names — order and spelling

- **Order: keep Hungarian order (family name first).** Verified: 연합뉴스 writes "오르반 빅토르", "머저르 페테르" (YNA); so do 노컷뉴스 (NOCUT), 세계일보 (SEGYE), 디지털타임스 (DT) and 나무위키 (NAMU). Outlets that flip to Western order exist — 뉴시스 "페테르 머저르", "빅토르 오르반" (NEWSIS), 프레시안 "빅토르 오르반" (PRESSIAN), 더구루 "라슬로 가이도스" (GURU-105537), and 지평 "페테르 머저르" but "오르반 빅토르" in the same text (JP-15129). Use Hungarian order throughout and give the Latin original once: 머저르 페테르(Magyar Péter).
- **Spelling: NIKL rules**, not English-style readings. Typical press deviations to avoid: 마자르/마저르 for Magyar (rule: 머저르), 가이도스 for Gajdos (rule: 거이도시), 이반차 for Iváncsa (rule: 이반처), 페스트 for Pest (rule: 페슈트). Keep the established exceptions: **부다페스트** (rule would give 부더페슈트), **다뉴브강** for Duna.
- Non-Hungarian names keep their own conventions: Chinese names by the Chinese rules, Hanja in brackets on first mention (청타오(成焘)); Western names in Western order.

### 1.2 Citing Hungarian legislation

Attested Korean practice is not uniform:

- 지평: "2018년 제 LVII호 법률", "정부령 561/2022 (XII.23.)", "헝가리 재무부령 제13/2024(VIII.15.)호" (JP-13793, JP-13953).
- KOTRA: Korean title + "(법률 CXII, 2011년)", "정부령 451/2016", "내무부령 25/2016" (KOTRA-2025).
- 세계법제정보센터 practice could not be checked (site refuses automated reading), so the "2013년 법률 제V호" pattern in the brief is **not verified** against it.

Recommended house style (B, consistent with both attested patterns):

| Hungarian form | Korean form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ÉÉÉÉ. évi N. törvény | 〈Korean title〉(ÉÉÉÉ년 법률 제N호, 약칭) — Roman numeral kept | 일반행정절차법(2016년 법률 제CL호, Ákr.) |
| nnn/ÉÉÉÉ. (H. N.) Korm. rendelet | 정부령 제nnn/ÉÉÉÉ(H. N.)호 | 정부령 제92/2026(VI. 5.)호 |
| nnnn/ÉÉÉÉ. (H. N.) Korm. határozat | 정부결정 제nnnn/ÉÉÉÉ(H. N.)호 | 정부결정 제1181/2026(VI. 5.)호 |
| miniszteri rendelet | 〈부처〉령 제n/ÉÉÉÉ(H. N.)호 | 재무부령 제13/2024(VIII. 15.)호 (A: JP-13953) |
| X. § (Y) bekezdés z) pont | 제X조 제Y항 (z)호 | Kp. 39. § (6) bek. → 행정소송법(Kp.) 제39조 제6항 |
| EU rendelet / irányelv | 규정 (EU) 2022/2560 / 지침 2010/75/EU | "규정 (EU) 2023/1542" attested (JP-13953) |

After the first full citation, use the Hungarian abbreviation alone (Ákr., Kp., Btk., Infotv., Üttv.) or the short Korean title.

### 1.3 Abbreviation on first mention

Korean name first, abbreviation in brackets, then the abbreviation or short Korean name: "헝가리 경쟁청(GVH)" → "GVH" / "경쟁청". Prefix "헝가리" on first mention of any national body. For bodies with no Korean precedent (B entries), add the Hungarian name once: "국가생활환경감독청(Országos Élőkörnyezet-felügyeleti Főhatóság)". Company-form suffixes stay in Latin inside company names ("Semcorp Hungary Kft.").

### 1.4 Currency and numbers

- **포린트** in running text (attested: JP-14130, GURU-106449; KOTRA writes "포린트화", "HUF"); **유로**. Do not use Ft or €.
- **Hungarian "millió/milliárd" must be converted to 만/억/조** — `milliárd` = 10억, not 1억.

| Hungarian | Korean |
|---|---|
| 1 millió Ft | 100만 포린트 |
| 10 millió Ft | 1,000만 포린트 |
| 34,5 millió Ft | 3,450만 포린트 |
| 100 millió Ft | 1억 포린트 |
| 405,3 millió Ft | 4억 530만 포린트 |
| 437 millió / 882 millió Ft | 4억 3,700만 / 8억 8,200만 포린트 |
| 500 millió Ft | 5억 포린트 |
| 5 milliárd Ft | 50억 포린트 |
| 16,4 milliárd euró | 164억 유로 |

- Attested models: "1억 포린트(약 4억3700만원)" (GURU-106449), "1조 6,450억 포린트" (JP-14130). A won equivalent in brackets is common in Korean press but needs a dated exchange rate; recommend omitting it or adding it once per article with the date.
- Decimal comma → point (0,5 százalék → 0.5%); thousands separator comma (13 000-szeres → 1만 3,000배). "9%-os" → 9%.

### 1.5 Dates

2026. június 24. → 2026년 6월 24일; ranges 2018~2025년; quarters 2026년 4분기; in tables "2026. 6. 24." is acceptable.

### 1.6 "Fine" — keep three words apart

Korean press mixes 벌금 / 과징금 / 과태료 for Hungarian `bírság` (벌금: GURU-105537, IMAEIL; 과징금: GURU-106449; 과태료: PRESSIAN). In a law-firm text 벌금 means a criminal penalty, so: administrative `bírság` → **과징금**; `eljárási bírság` → **절차 과태료**; `pénzbírság` under Act CIV of 2001 (criminal measure against a legal person) → **벌금**.
