### 1.2 CONFUSION RISK — 德恒 (DeHeng Law Offices) is a different firm and already "owns" the Hangul forms
- 德恒 (DeHeng Law Offices, dehenglaw.com — note: **dehenglaw.com is 德恒's domain, not DHH's**; DHH's domains are deheheng.com / en.deheheng.com) is rendered in Korean press as **"더헝"**: "중국 더헝(德恒律师事务所 | DeHeng Law Offices)로펌 칭다오사무소" — Herald Business, 2026-01-27: https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10663281
- 德恒 **does** have a Seoul office (reported by ALB China: "德恒律师事务所正式落子首尔,成为该所全球范围内第51家分支机构 … 德恒首尔办公室将由合伙人祝翠瑛律师负责领导"): https://china.legalbusinessonline.com/index.php/other-news/%E5%BE%B7%E6%81%92%E3%80%81%E9%94%A6%E5%A4%A9%E5%9F%8E%E8%BF%9B%E4%B8%80%E6%AD%A5%E5%BC%80%E6%8B%93%E4%B8%9C%E4%BA%9A%E5%B8%83%E5%B1%80%EF%BC%88zhen%EF%BC%89
- A Korean search hit titled "법무법인 덕형 (중국 칭다오)" exists (Pusan National University law school internship notice); the page itself returned an error, so I could not determine whether "덕형" there is 德恒 or 德衡 (Shandong Deheng, DHH's sister firm). **[NOT VERIFIED]** https://graduate.pusan.ac.kr/law/19752/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGYmJzJTJGbGF3JTJGMzg3NSUyRjE2OTYzNTElMkZhcnRjbFZpZXcuZG8lM0Y%3D
- Consequence: a Mandarin-based Hangul form "더허헝" differs from 德恒's "더헝" by one syllable, and a Sino-Korean form "덕화형" sits next to "덕형/덕항". Any Hangul-only rendering invites exactly the 德恒 confusion. Also 德衡 (Shandong Deheng) and 德恒 are **homophones in Mandarin (Déhéng)**, so both would be "더헝" in Hangul.

### 1.3 Recommendation
- Use the firm's own Latin brand plus hanja on first mention, with the generic noun 로펌 (press convention for foreign firms, see §5): **"베이징 DHH 로펌(北京德和衡律师事务所, Beijing DHH Law Firm)"**, thereafter **"DHH"** / **"DHH 로펌"**.
- Do not publish "더허헝" or "덕화형" as if they were established names. If a pronunciation aid is wanted, the Mandarin-based "더허헝" follows the Korean loanword rules for Chinese (de→더, he→허, heng→헝) — my own derivation, **[NOT VERIFIED against any real usage]**; best to ask DHH Beijing whether an official Korean name exists.
- Never write "더헝" or "DeHeng" for DHH.

### 3.2 Recommended labels (each attested above unless marked)
- About: **법인소개** is the big-firm norm but presupposes a 법무법인; for a 법률사무소 use **사무소 소개** (or simply **소개**, used as a sub-item by BKL and as a section heading by Kim & Chang). "사무소 소개" as an exact menu label: **[NOT VERIFIED on a specific site]**, built from attested 소개 + 사무소.
- Practice areas: **업무분야** (universal).
- People: **구성원** (universal; "전문가" is not used as the menu label by any of the six).
- Insights/News: **소식/자료** (지평), 소식자료 (BKL), 뉴스레터, 법률정보 (지평), 언론보도, 기고. For a blog: "소식·자료" or "법률정보".
- Contact: **연락처**; directions: **오시는 길**; office location: **사무소 위치**; inquiry: 문의.
- Skip link: **본문 바로가기** (Kim & Chang, Yulchon) / 본문으로 바로가기 (Hwawoo).
- Read more: **더 보기** (Kim & Chang) / 더보기 (BKL, Hwawoo, Yulchon). Standard spacing per Korean orthography is "더 보기"; "자세히 보기" **[NOT VERIFIED on these sites]**.
- Back to list: **목록** (BKL; Hwawoo uses English "BACK TO LIST"). "목록으로" is common Korean web usage **[NOT VERIFIED on these sites]**.
- Privacy policy: **개인정보 처리방침** (Hwawoo spelling; this is also the statutory term, 개인정보 보호법 제30조 — statute cited from my own knowledge, **[statute text not fetched]**) / 개인정보처리방침 (Kim & Chang, BKL, Shin & Kim, Yulchon, Jipyong — unspaced variant is more common on sites).
- Legal notice / disclaimer: **면책공고** (Shin & Kim, Hwawoo, Jipyong) or **법적고지** (Kim & Chang, BKL).
- Pro bono: **공익활동** is the profession's term (BKL: "공익활동위원회"); the menu-level label on big-firm sites is usually **사회공헌** (Kim & Chang, BKL, Shin & Kim, Hwawoo).
- Careers: 인재채용 (Kim & Chang, BKL) / 인재영입 (Shin & Kim, Jipyong).
- Share/print: 공유하기, 프린트 / 인쇄하기, PDF 다운로드, URL 복사.
- Book a consultation: **상담 예약** — not used by the top-six firm sites (they have no booking CTA); it is ordinary Korean and typical of smaller firms' sites. **[NOT VERIFIED on a specific law-firm site]**. Alternatives: "상담 문의", "문의하기".
- Cookie banner: none of the Korean firm sites checked shows a cookie banner (not customary in Korea), so there is **no law-firm-specific convention [NOT VERIFIED]**. Attested generic Korean cookie wording (official Czech tourism site, ko-KR): heading "우리는 귀하의 개인 정보를 존중합니다"; "당사는 콘텐츠 개인화, 트래픽 분석 … 쿠키를 사용합니다."; button "모두 허용" / "모든 쿠키 허용"; categories 필수 / 분석 / 통계 / 마케팅: https://www.visitczechia.com/ko-kr/cookies . Suggested set (ordinary Korean, my composition): "쿠키 설정", "모두 허용", "모두 거부", "필수 쿠키만 허용", "설정 저장" **[composition, NOT VERIFIED verbatim]**.
- "First published in" → "최초 게재: …" / "원문 게재처: …"; "Last updated" → "최종 업데이트: YYYY.MM.DD" / "최종 수정일". **[NOT VERIFIED on these sites — they show a bare date such as "2026.09.30" without label (BKL)]**. Date format on Korean firm sites: YYYY.MM.DD.

### 3.5 Foreign names
- Korean firm sites keep foreign lawyers' names in **Latin script**, title in Korean: "Moritz Winkler 외국변호사" (Yulchon Germany page, https://www.yulchon.com/ko/expertise/practices/germany/1249/page.do); for ethnic-Korean/Chinese lawyers: Hangul + Latin in brackets: "구연니 (Yan Ni QIU) 외국변호사" (Kim & Chang), "신준호 (Philippe Shin) 외국변호사" (Shin & Kim, https://shinkim.com/kor/business/view/U1098); Baker Tilly: "Dr. Raphael Won-Pil Suh (서원필)".

## 5. 법률사무소 vs 법무법인 vs 로펌; rendering "Czudar DHH Ügyvédi Iroda"

### 5.1 Usage
- **법무법인** = a specific Korean statutory entity form (법무법인, 법무법인(유한)); Korean firms carry it in their names ("법무법인(유) 세종", "법무법인(유한) 태평양"). Applying it to a foreign firm asserts a Korean legal form — avoid. (One counter-example: Jipyong's page loosely says "헝가리 법무법인".)
- **로펌** = the generic noun Korean press and firms use for foreign firms: "헝가리 로펌 오펜하임(Oppenheim)" (https://www.inews24.com/view/1777393 ; 아주경제 via https://jipyong.com/kr/board/press_view.php?seq=13523), "헝가리의 명문 로펌인 오펜하임(Oppenheim)" (Jipyong), "중국 로펌", "싱가포르 대형 로펌 '왕파트너십'", "유럽 지역 로펌" (BKL). It is descriptive, not normally part of a formal name.
- **법률사무소** = neutral "law office"; used in formal names (김·장 법률사무소) and by Korean press when translating a foreign firm's own "律师事务所"-type name: "中 중성청태법률사무소" (https://www.fnnews.com/news/202403251456559546). Also the statutory term for licensed foreign firms' Korean branches: "외국법자문법률사무소는 본점사무소의 명칭 다음에 "외국법자문법률사무소"를 덧붙인 명칭을 사용하여야 한다" (외국법자문사법 제27조 제2항, https://www.koreanbar.or.kr/pages/file/FLC/외국법자문사법(법률 10542, 2011. 4. 30 시행).pdf — 2011 version; current wording not re-checked).
- "율사사무소" (literal Sino-Korean of 律师事务所): no usage surfaced in the sources fetched; do not use.
- → "ügyvédi iroda" = **법률사무소** in the formal name; **"헝가리 로펌"** as the descriptive apposition.

### 5.2 Recommended rendering
- Formal name: **"Czudar DHH 법률사무소"** (brand in Latin, as Korean sites do for foreign names).
- First mention / About page: **"Czudar DHH 법률사무소(Czudar DHH Ügyvédi Iroda)"**, described as "헝가리 부다페스트 소재 로펌" and "베이징 DHH 로펌(北京德和衡律师事务所)의 부다페스트 사무소".
- Thereafter: "Czudar DHH" / "당 사무소" (self-reference; big Korean firms say "당 법인", which fits only a 법인).

### 5.3 Hangul for "Czudar" — correction to the "추다르" candidate
- Official loanword rules for Hungarian (문화부 고시 제1992-31호, 동구권 5개 언어; table reproduced at https://pyogi.kkeutsori.com/%ED%98%84%ED%96%89-%EC%99%B8%EB%9E%98%EC%96%B4-%ED%91%9C%EA%B8%B0%EB%B2%95/%ED%97%9D%EA%B0%80%EB%A6%AC%EC%96%B4/ — a secondary reproduction; the korean.go.kr / wikisource originals could not be fetched **[primary text NOT VERIFIED directly]**):
  - c → ㅊ before a vowel ("citrom 치트롬"); d → ㄷ ("daru 더루"); r → 르 word-finally ("ár 아르"); u → 우; **short a → 어** ("lakat 러커트"), long á → 아 ("máj 마이"); zs → 주 word-finally ("rozs 로주").
  - The table has no "cz" row; cz is the archaic surname spelling of c /ts/ — treating it as c is my inference **[NOT VERIFIED in the rules]**.
- Result under the official rules: **Czudar → "추더르"**, not "추다르" (unaccented a = 어). By the same table, Balázs → "벌라주" (my derivation, **[NOT VERIFIED against usage]**).
- Real-world practice deviates: Korean press writes Hungarian a as 아 ("사트마리" for Szatmáry, https://www.inews24.com/view/1777393), and the same reference page itself criticises the rule ("a를 '어'로 적기로 한 선택은 재검토할 필요가 있다"). So "추다르" is what a Korean reader/journalist would spontaneously write; "추더르" is the normative form.
- Recommendation: keep **Latin "Czudar" as the name everywhere**; add a Hangul gloss **once** as a pronunciation aid only — "Czudar(추더르) DHH 법률사무소" if following the National Institute of Korean Language rule. Do not make Hangul part of the brand. Whether to prefer 추더르 (normative) or 추다르 (intuitive) is a brand decision for the firm.

## Citation style for GDPR articles

- **PIPC/KISA translation style [GT-13], [GT-17], [GT-46]:** cross-references to other articles as "제6조(1)의 (f)호", "제9조(2)의 (a)호", "제21조(1)", "제45조(3)", "제22조(1) 및 (4)"; inside the same article "제1항 (f)호" [GT-6]. Points are listed as "(a)"…"(f)" and cited with the suffix "호".
- **Korean legal-drafting style in secondary sources:** "GDPR 제6조 제1항 (e)호", "GDPR 제7조 제3항" [NEPLA]; "GDPR 제12조 1항" [MONO].
- Conclusion: "GDPR 제6조 제1항 (b)호" is correct, natural Korean and consistent with the translation's in-article form ("제1항 (f)호"); the translation's own cross-article form is "제6조(1)의 (b)호". Either is defensible; pick one and keep it consistent. Whether the KISA PDF itself is typeset identically to gdpr-text.com: **not verified**.
